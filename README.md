# OurHome - Mortgage & Paycheck Planner 🏡

A modern mortgage calculator designed for couples and smart homeowners looking to pay off their home faster and align their mortgage with their paychecks.

## Key Features

1. **Extra Payments (Interest Shredder)**:
   - Pay additional principal every month.
   - Pay additional lump sum each year (e.g. tax refund or annual bonus).
   - Custom one-time future lump-sum payments.
2. **Paycheck Frequency Strategy**:
   - Standard Monthly (12/yr).
   - **Accelerated Bi-Weekly (26/yr)**: Pay half the monthly mortgage every 2 weeks — automatically making 13 full payments per year and shaving 4–6 years off a 30-year mortgage!
   - Semi-Monthly (24/yr).
   - Regular Bi-Weekly & Weekly.
3. **Interactive Amortization Visuals**:
   - **Principal vs Interest**: Stacked bar chart showing the shift from interest to principal over time.
   - **Balance & Equity Trajectory**: Area curves tracking remaining loan balance vs growing home equity.
   - **Total Interest Paid**: Cumulative interest comparison showing exact money saved.
4. **Comprehensive Loan & Housing Costs**:
   - Property Taxes, Homeowners Insurance, HOA dues, and PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance with auto-detection for <20% down).
5. **Couple Income & Paycheck Split**:
   - Split payments 50/50, proportionally by income, or custom split to see each partner's share per paycheck.
6. **Schedule Export**:
   - Annual summary or detailed payment breakdown exportable directly to CSV (Excel / Google Sheets).
